Questions

Straight answers.

01How does Bunya help with annual reviews and fee consent?
Bunya Command Centre starts each review cycle from the client’s dates, prepares what has changed since the last review, and keeps fee consent with the review it belongs to. Your team approves everything that goes to the client.
02How far ahead does a review cycle start?
As far ahead as your firm chooses. The trigger date and every step after it are agreed in your Blueprint.
03Does anything go to the client without a person?
No. Invitations, follow-ups and consent requests are drafted for your team. A person checks and approves each one before it goes.
04Does Bunya make sure we meet our fee consent obligations?
No software can do that for you. Bunya keeps consent status with each review, reminds the right person and keeps the evidence. Your firm remains responsible for its obligations.
