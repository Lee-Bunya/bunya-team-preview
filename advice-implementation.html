Questions

Straight answers.

01What is advice implementation?
It is the work between the client signing the advice and the advice being in place: authorities, forms, product actions and confirmations. In Bunya Command Centre each action has an owner, a due date and a person who authorises it.
02Does Bunya submit applications to product providers?
No. Bunya prepares the paperwork and the checklist. Your team authorises each action and completes it through your usual channels.
03Can we use our own implementation checklists?
Yes. Your checklists, task owners and due-date rules are agreed in your Blueprint, so implementation follows how your firm already works.
04When is the first review set?
The first annual review date is proposed as the advice is put in place and confirmed by the adviser, so the review cycle starts from a real date.
