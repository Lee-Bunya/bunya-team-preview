01 / THE STARTING POINT

Less piecing together.
More time for clients.

Regional Prosperity Financial Advice helps regional people, families and communities with financial advice. With approximately 20 full-time team members and offices in Colac, Hamilton, Geelong, Warrnambool and Queenscliff, the firm wanted to make client information easier to use across its advice process.

Previously, documents were organised by adviser, with separate folders for clients, prospects and archived records. Advice preparation involved manually bringing together information from Word documents, Excel and reports from Xplan.

The goal was a more automated, consistent process that gave advisers more time with clients without losing the checks that good advice requires.

02 / THE APPROACH

Build on the environment
the team already uses.

Bunya appealed because it could connect information and advice workflows within Regional Prosperity’s existing Microsoft environment, including Teams and SharePoint. For Kane, the attraction was a practical approach tailored to financial advice, with adviser judgement and review remaining central.

The work has included CRM configuration, a staff briefing, SharePoint structure, filing automation and wider workflow integrations. Kane has used Command Centre and explored the fact-find-to-SOA request workflow.

Implementation has involved collaborative workshops and iterative testing. Agreeing folder names, mapping the filing structure and connecting the wider workflow have required practical work from both advisers and administration staff.

03 / A WORKFLOW IN PRACTICE

From existing information
to an SOA request.

Before: preparing an SOA request meant manually collating client information and completing the request.

With Bunya: Kane describes using a one-click fact find to synthesise existing data, then lodging the SOA request through the connected workflow.

This is data preparation and request lodgement—not a completed or approved Statement of Advice. The adviser still checks completeness, recommendations and the final document.

04 / KANE’S PERSPECTIVE

Technology that supports
the adviser relationship.

“BunyaAI is helping us work smarter so we can spend more time with clients.”

Kane Leersen
Organisation Leader & Personal Financial Adviser
Regional Prosperity Financial Advice

Kane sees judgement and trust at the core of the adviser–client relationship. The early value is making existing information more usable in the advice workflow, with a longer-term ambition to improve the client experience.

05 / THE EARLY VALUE

A clearer view of work
and capacity.

Kane identifies value in understanding where the business is at: the workloads individuals are carrying and the ability to align resources where they can have the greatest impact.

These are the firm’s reported observations. Regional Prosperity has not established a verified before-and-after baseline or production average, so this story does not claim a measured time saving or quantified productivity increase.

06 / ADVICE FOR OTHER FIRMS

Start with one real use case.

Kane’s advice is practical: start with a clear process, bring advisers and administration into the design, test it with real work and keep human review in place.

For Regional Prosperity, connecting information is a means to a more useful advice workflow—and a stronger focus on the people the firm serves.
