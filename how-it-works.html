01 / BLUEPRINT

See the plan.
Before the build.

Map your current processes and explore a clickable model of the system. The Blueprint defines your product scope, connections, responsibilities and delivery plan.

UNDERSTAND

Map the real work.

Review client journeys, team handovers, existing tools and the points where work gets held up.

SHAPE

Choose what fits.

Agree the role of Command Centre, Broadcast HQ and Bunya Voice, with the workflows and integrations your firm needs.

AGREE

Make the scope clear.

Confirm costs, timing, data migration, licensing and responsibilities. Keep your Blueprint either way; its cost is credited if you build.

02 / BUILD & ROLLOUT

Configure it together.
Prove it in practice.

Your team helps shape the system as it is built. Test the agreed workflows and prepare people for the move into daily use.

Build around your firm

Configure workflows, branding, roles and permissions in your Microsoft environment.

Connect and migrate

Agree source data, mapping and validation. Configure the integrations included in your scope.

Test with your people

Check the client journeys, outputs and approval points with the team who will use them.

Train and transition

Prepare tailored guidance, run-in support and a rollout plan that suits the practice.

03 / OWNERSHIP

Built in your Microsoft.
Owned by your firm.

The system is built around your processes in your firm’s Microsoft tenancy. Your data, access and ways of working stay under your control.

Your environment

Use your Microsoft foundation, with access configured for the people who need it.

Your operating model

Shape the stages, responsibilities and approval rules around the firm’s agreed processes.

Clear ongoing costs

Ownership still involves Microsoft licensing and agreed support or services. These are confirmed alongside the build scope.

04 / ONGOING SUPPORT

Live is the beginning.
Support continues.

Agree how Bunya will support the system after rollout, including monitoring, training, maintenance and the process for requesting changes.

Help when the team needs it

Define the support arrangement and the route for raising questions or issues.

Guidance for daily use

Keep documentation and training relevant to the system your team actually uses.

Keep connections working

Confirm monitoring and maintenance responsibilities for the services and automations in scope.

Plan the next improvement

Review change requests and agree additional work before extending the system.

Scope, timing, licensing, service allowances and costs are agreed for your firm.
