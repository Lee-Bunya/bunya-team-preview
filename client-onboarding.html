How it works

Four phases. Who does what at each one.

  1. Engage

    Getting to know the client

    Bunya prepares

    A draft file note from the first call, and fact-find inputs from discovery.

    Your team decides

    Checks the note and confirms the fact find with the client.

  2. Strategy

    Working out the advice

    Bunya prepares

    Modelling inputs and a branded strategy presentation with the fee quote.

    Your team decides

    The adviser applies strategy judgement and presents it.

  3. Activation

    From go-ahead to the SoA

    Bunya prepares

    An SoA request pack from the recorded discussion.

    Your team decides

    The client says go. The adviser checks the pack before it moves on.

  4. Implementation

    Putting the advice in place

    Bunya prepares

    Authority paperwork, implementation tasks and the first review date.

    Your team decides

    The client authorises. The adviser signs off each action.

Questions

Straight answers.

01What is client onboarding software for financial advisers?
It is software that takes a new client from first contact to implemented advice. In Bunya Command Centre, every stage has an owner, the evidence it needs and a clear next step, inside your firm’s own Microsoft 365.
02Do we have to use these stages?
No. This is an example. Your stages, names and approval points are agreed in your Blueprint, so the journey matches how your firm already works.
03Does Bunya move a client to the next stage on its own?
No. Bunya prepares the work for each stage. A person on your team checks it, decides and moves the client on.
04Do we need Bunya Voice to capture meetings?
Bunya Voice is an optional add-on, and Command Centre supports the onboarding journey without it.
