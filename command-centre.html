02 / Follow the client

One relationship.
A connected journey.

Follow the same client context from enquiry through advice and the next review. These walkthroughs use example data; your Blueprint defines the stages, responsibilities and automation rules for your firm.

Start with the person, not another spreadsheet.

Capture the enquiry and connect people, households and related entities. Give the team a shared starting point for the conversation.

  • Relationships and contact details
  • Owner and next action

Turn the first conversation into a clear handover.

Bring documents and meeting context together. Identify what is missing and assign the preparation needed before advice work moves forward.

  • Documents and meeting context
  • Drafts for human review

Prepare the advice.
Make the handover clear.

Bring client information, documents and meeting context into advice preparation. Define the fact-find, Statement of Advice (SoA) and Record of Advice (RoA) drafting workflows your firm needs, including templates, required inputs and human review.

  • Check inputs and unresolved questions before handover
  • Track responsibility, drafts and review stages
  • Confirm supported document outputs in your build scope

Keep the follow-through attached to the advice.

Track the actions that follow approved advice. Keep the relevant tasks and supporting documents with the matter so the next person has the context.

  • Assigned implementation tasks
  • Supporting documents

Make the next review
part of the workflow.

Configure review cycles around agreed dates and rules, with preparation tasks and owners defined for the next cycle. Keep the relationship history close to the work and make overdue or incomplete items visible.

  • Agree date triggers, task creation and responsibilities
  • Coordinate preparation, meetings and follow-up
  • Check exceptions when dates or circumstances change

SERVICE DELIVERY & EVIDENCE

Keep the evidence
with the work.

A completed meeting, an outstanding action and an approved document tell different parts of the story. Keep the supporting context connected so another authorised person can follow what happened and what remains to be done.

AGREED WORK

Know what is due.

Define how agreed services, review dates and responsibilities are represented against the client. Give the team a practical starting point for checking delivery.

SUPPORTING RECORDS

Find what supports it.

Connect relevant activities, documents and reviewed outputs with the matter, rather than relying on someone to reconstruct the history later.

EXCEPTIONS

Keep unfinished work visible.

Distinguish completed activity from outstanding follow-up. Assign the next action when evidence is missing or work has changed.

Your scope defines the evidence fields, approval history, access and retention. An organised record supports professional review; it does not itself establish compliance or prove that every obligation has been met.

04 / THE AI LAYER

Preparation can move faster.
Decisions stay with people.

Use the client context already connected to your practice to prepare drafts and carry out agreed tasks. Skills give repeatable work a defined method; approval points keep your team in control.

CONTEXT

Start with the relevant records.

Work with connected client information and Microsoft documents within the firm’s configured permissions.

SKILLS

Give repeatable work a method.

Run agreed playbooks with defined inputs, steps and review points, from preparation to handover.

ACTIONS

Ask for the next step.

Prepare drafts, jobs or record updates in plain English. Confirm write actions before saving, and review material before it is sent.

ILLUSTRATIVE REQUEST / CONFIGURED AI SKILL

“Prepare a review job for this client.”

Prepare: use the permitted client context to propose the job and tasks. Review: check the client, dates and responsibilities before confirming the write. Record: retain the resulting work and the agreed approval information with the record.

The enabled actions and recorded events depend on your configured skill and governance requirements. Ask to see those steps in your demonstration.
