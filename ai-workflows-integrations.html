Questions

Straight answers.

01What is an AI workflow in Bunya Command Centre?
A repeatable recipe for one piece of prework, such as a meeting file note or an SoA request pack. It states what it uses, what it prepares and who checks it, so the work is prepared the same way every time. Bunya calls these Skills.
02Can AI send anything or change our records on its own?
No. AI-prepared work waits for a person before it is used or actioned. Your team edits it, approves it or sends it back, and advisers keep the strategy and product judgement.
03Is the AI always right?
No, and we don’t claim it is. That is why every workflow ends with a named person checking the output against the source.
04Where does our data go when AI is used?
The AI services, data location and any connected third-party processing are reviewed as part of the solution design for your firm.
05Which specialist tools can you connect?
Your Blueprint looks at the tools your firm uses now and names which to keep, connect or retire. Where a supported connection exists, we connect it; where it does not, we say so.
06Do we have to replace our current advice software?
Not automatically. Your Blueprint identifies what to keep, connect or replace, including specialist advice tools.
