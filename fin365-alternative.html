01 / THE SHORT ANSWER

Microsoft is the start.
The scope is the decision.

Fin365 documents a broad financial planning software offering. Bunya combines client operations, campaigns and conversations in a build scoped for the firm. Compare demonstrated coverage and delivery terms rather than product labels.

02 / START WITH YOUR REQUIREMENTS

Two proposals.
One brief from your firm.

Give both suppliers the same workflows, required data feeds and acceptance criteria. Ask them to distinguish included capabilities from configuration, custom work and retained tools.

EVALUATING FIN365

Test the existing product against your work.

Ask how its client management, reporting, documents and integrations handle your specific scenarios. Confirm what configuration and additional services are needed.

EVALUATING BUNYA

Test the proposed operating system.

Ask how Command Centre, Broadcast HQ and Bunya Voice will connect for your firm. Confirm the build boundaries, migration requirements and ongoing support.

If you already use Fin365, compare improving the current configuration with moving. A replacement needs to justify migration effort, retraining and any lost or retained functionality. See the build-versus-buy guide.

03 / MAKE THE DEMO PRACTICAL

Follow the work.
Check the handovers.

Use fictional or otherwise authorised sample data. Ask each supplier to demonstrate these scenarios in the proposed configuration.

  1. Run a client review.

    Find the household, open the relevant records, assign work and check how overdue or incomplete tasks are handled.

  2. Trace a figure to its source.

    Choose an external account value or revenue item. Confirm the feed, update timing, reconciliation and response to missing data.

  3. Prepare and review a document.

    Use your intended template and approval process. Check where drafts, corrections and final records are stored.

  4. Test a campaign or call workflow.

    Where required, show how a campaign is approved or a conversation links to the client. Identify additional products and licences.

  5. Check control and continuity.

    Demonstrate permissions, exports and handover. Confirm who maintains customisations and what happens when you change supplier.

Compare the full cost over the same period.

Include implementation, migration, licences, usage, retained tools, training and support. This research does not establish equivalent current quotes or a price winner. Use the total cost checklist.

04 / COMMON QUESTIONS

Get the terms
and details in writing.

The shared technology foundation does not settle the implementation, ownership or cost questions.

Is Fin365 just a basic CRM?

No. Its published offering extends to workflow, revenue, documents, reporting and portal capabilities. Evaluate those against your requirements. See Fin365’s feature overview.

Does Bunya have all the same integrations?

Do not assume that. List the feeds and systems your firm relies on and confirm the scope of each connection before deciding.

Can we move from Fin365 to Bunya?

A migration needs assessment of export rights, data structure, documents, relationships and retained capabilities. Agree a sample transfer and validation criteria before committing. See the migration checklist.

Does using our Microsoft environment mean we own everything?

No. Data access, tenancy administration, application rights and ownership of custom work are distinct. Check the contracts for each proposal, including third-party components and exit arrangements.

Is Bunya cheaper or more capable?

This comparison does not establish either claim. Ask for equivalent scope, current pricing and a demonstration of your critical workflows. Assess any gaps and ongoing responsibilities alongside the price.
