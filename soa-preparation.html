Questions

Straight answers.

01What does SoA preparation software do?
It prepares the work that goes into a Statement of Advice: fact-find inputs, documents, the strategy discussion and modelling notes, assembled into one request pack. In Bunya Command Centre the adviser checks the pack before it goes to paraplanning.
02Does Bunya write the SoA for us?
No. Bunya prepares the inputs and the request. Your paraplanners and advisers produce, review and present the advice.
03Does it work with our current SoA and modelling tools?
Your Blueprint identifies which tools to keep and which to connect. Supported connections are agreed for each firm.
04Who approves the request before it goes to paraplanning?
The adviser. The request pack is checked and approved, edited or sent back before anything moves on. A clickthrough is never advice approval.
