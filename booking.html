Questions

Straight answers.

01Is the demo free?
Yes. The demo is one free meeting with no obligation. You only commit to a paid step after you have seen a written proposal.
02What happens in the meeting?
We show you the advice journey in Command Centre with example data, and you tell us how your firm works today and where advice waits. It covers the demo and discovery in one meeting.
03Who should attend?
The principal, and anyone else you would like there, such as an adviser, paraplanner or practice manager.
04What should we bring?
One workflow that slows your advice down, the systems it touches and the outcome you want to change. There is no need to bring client records.
05What happens after the demo?
If it looks like a fit, you receive a written proposal with scope options and pricing. You then decide whether to start a Blueprint. How getting started works ↗
