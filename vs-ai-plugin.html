01 / THE SHORT ANSWER

Decide what you need to improve.

An AI assistant may be a suitable way to support a focused task in your existing tools. Its access, actions and outputs depend on the product, integrations and configuration. Assess those capabilities directly.

Bunya’s proposition includes the operating workflow around AI: client records and work in Command Centre, campaigns in Broadcast HQ, and connected conversations through Bunya Voice. Inclusion, permissions and review points are agreed in your build.

An assistant is not necessarily limited to drafting, and a Bunya build does not automatically replace every application. This guide compares decision criteria rather than claiming universal feature differences.

02 / CHOOSE THE RIGHT SCOPE

A focused addition or a wider change?

A focused AI addition may fit

Your current workflow is effective and you want help with a defined activity. Test the assistant against that task, including integration effort, corrections and ongoing usage.

A wider operating-system build may fit

The problem spans records, ownership of tasks, handovers and disconnected processes. Test the proposed system as well as the AI outputs.

03 / TEST THE WHOLE TASK

From context to a reviewed result.

  1. Check the source information

    Which records are accessible? How are missing information, permissions and conflicting sources handled?

  2. Follow actions and approvals

    Show proposed updates, review steps and how incorrect outputs are corrected. Confirm what can happen automatically.

  3. Inspect the resulting record

    Where are the approved output, actions and supporting context stored? Can the next team member follow the work?

  4. Compare support and change

    Who maintains the connections, instructions and workflows when the underlying tools change?

Compare the full commitment.

Include implementation, migration, licences, usage, retained tools, training and support. Customer data, tenancy control, application licences and ownership of custom work are different matters. Check each proposal and contract.
