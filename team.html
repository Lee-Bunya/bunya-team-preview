Who we are

Financial advice software. For Australian advice firms.

Bunya Command Centre helps take clients from first meeting to implemented advice, with AI preparing the work and advisers approving every step. It is built in your firm’s own Microsoft 365, around the way your firm already works. Bunya Voice and Broadcast HQ are optional add-ons when you are ready for more.

Start with the real work

Understand the people, information and exceptions before deciding what to automate.

Human approval is a gate

Prove it together

Test agreed scenarios with the people who will use the system. Give gaps a clear owner before rollout.
