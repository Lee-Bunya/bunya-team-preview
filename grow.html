03 / FROM BRIEF TO HANDOVER

A clear sequence.
A team ready to use it.

Start with the gaps that matter to your firm. Build and review the work together, then give your team the material and guidance to apply it.

  1. 01 / DISCOVER

    Understand your starting point.

    Review your audience, existing brand, website and content. Identify what is working and where people struggle to understand your offer.

  2. 02 / AGREE

    Define the work and responsibilities.

    Build, review and refine.

    Develop the agreed brand and website work, then prepare the campaign setup. Your team checks accuracy, tone and required approvals.

  4. 04 / APPLY

    Learn the process and use it.

    Work through the first campaigns together. Bunya’s client video tutorials support learning and practical application; agree any additional training and support in the proposal.

05 / BEFORE YOU START

Know what you’re
building towards.

A clear scope makes the service easier to plan, review and run after handover.

Is Grow included in the Bunya operating system?

No. Grow is an optional service for brand, website and marketing setup. It is scoped separately from the core platform, including any Broadcast HQ requirements.

Do we need to replace our existing brand and website?

Not necessarily. Start with a review of what you have. The proposal can identify what to retain, refine or rebuild based on the agreed requirements.

Will you write the website content?

Website copy can form part of the agreed work. Your team supplies accurate information about the firm and reviews claims, services and required disclosures before publication.

Does Grow guarantee enquiries, rankings or AI visibility?

No. Grow focuses on the brand, website and content foundations within the agreed scope. Search placement, AI references and commercial outcomes cannot be guaranteed.

Who approves AI-generated marketing content?

Your authorised people review the material under the firm’s approval process. Agree the roles and review rules during setup. AI prepares drafts; it does not replace that responsibility.

What do we receive at handover?

The proposal defines the brand assets, website access, documentation and training supplied. Confirm ownership and usage rights, third-party licences, hosting, maintenance and account responsibilities in the agreement.

Can you help after the initial setup?

Discuss the level of ongoing help your team needs. Confirm any managed service, content support or additional work separately, including responsibilities and recurring costs.
