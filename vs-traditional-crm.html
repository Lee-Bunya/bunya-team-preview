01 / THE SHORT ANSWER

A product label does not settle the scope.

Advice CRM offerings vary. Assess workflow, documents, reporting and integrations in the actual product and configuration under consideration. Do not assume that a CRM only stores contacts, or that Bunya replaces every specialist tool.

Bunya brings Command Centre, Broadcast HQ and Bunya Voice into an agreed operating system for your firm. The Blueprint defines which products, connections and workflows are included.

This is an evaluation guide prepared by Bunya, a supplier. It is not a feature ranking of every CRM or a claim that one approach is always better.

02 / TWO USEFUL STARTING POINTS

Improve what works.
Understand what needs to change.

Evaluate the current CRM

Can configuration, training or an integration solve the bottleneck? Consider existing investment, familiar workflows and specialist capabilities before choosing to move.

Evaluate a Bunya build

Demonstrate how client work, campaigns and conversations would connect in the agreed scope. Identify what is available, what needs configuration and what requires further assessment.

03 / USE THE SAME BRIEF

Make the comparison practical.

  1. Follow a complete client review

    Find the household, assign preparation, record decisions and track follow-up. Test overdue work and exceptions, not just the ideal path.

  2. Check specialist requirements

    List modelling, research, feeds, document templates and other tools you rely on. Confirm what stays and how information moves.

  3. Inspect AI and approval

    Ask what context an assistant can access, which actions it can take and where a person reviews the result.

  4. Plan the move and the handover

    Assess exports, data quality, migration validation, training and responsibilities after go-live.

Compare the full commitment.

Include implementation, migration, licences, usage, retained tools, training and support. Customer data, tenancy control, application licences and ownership of custom work are different matters. Check each proposal and contract.
