What changes

The meeting is the easy part. The follow-up is where it slips.

After a client conversation, someone has to write it up, file it and follow through. Bunya Voice starts that work from the conversation itself, and your team finishes it.

Questions

Straight answers.

01What is Bunya Voice?
An optional add-on to Bunya Command Centre. Supported calls and Teams meetings are captured and filed with the client, and your team gets meeting notes, actions and a branded follow-up email draft to check.
02Do we need Bunya Voice to use Command Centre?
No. Command Centre supports the advice journey from first meeting to implemented advice without it, and advice delivery does not depend on Bunya Voice. Your Blueprint confirms whether it is included for your firm.
03Does anything go to the client without a person checking it?
No. Notes, actions and the follow-up email are drafts for your team to check against the conversation before anything is relied on or sent.
04How are access and recordings handled?
Recording requirements, consent, permissions, storage and retention are agreed before rollout, and access to the records is tested before go-live. See Security & your data.
05Can it capture mobile calls?
Supported call and meeting sources are agreed for your firm. Ask about mobile calls in your demo.
06What affects scope and ongoing costs?
Users, number portability, call types, Microsoft and carrier licensing, recording services, AI usage and storage. Implementation, managed service and third-party charges are confirmed in your Blueprint.
