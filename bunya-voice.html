01 / FROM CONVERSATION TO ACTION

One conversation.
A connected path.

Bring the call, the record and the account together. Where the workflow cannot confidently match a conversation, your team reviews the exception.

  1. 01 / CONNECT

    Start in Teams.

    Connect or port eligible business numbers and configure in-scope licensed users.

  2. 02 / CAPTURE

    Keep the record.

    Capture eligible calls and meetings under agreed recording policies and consent settings.

  3. 03 / ORGANISE

    File it in SharePoint.

    Store supported recordings and transcripts with consistent structure and metadata.

  4. 04 / MATCH

    Link the account.

    Match the conversation to the relevant Power Platform account, with exceptions surfaced for review.

  5. 05 / PREPARE

    Draft the next step.

    For eligible client meetings, prepare notes, actions and a branded follow-up email for review.

What affects scope and ongoing costs?

Users, numbers, portability, call types, licensing, recording services, AI consumption, platform usage and storage are confirmed in the final scope. Microsoft and carrier charges are separate from implementation and the managed service.
