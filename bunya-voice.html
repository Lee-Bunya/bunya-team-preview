BEFORE YOU MAKE THE MOVE

Good questions.
Clear starting points.

What does our team receive?

The agreed workflow can provide the recording and transcript, meeting notes and actions, and a branded email draft. Confirm the supported call and meeting scenarios in your scope.

How are access and recordings handled?

Agree recording requirements, permissions, storage and retention before rollout. Test who can access the records and how they connect to the client workflow.

How do we get started?

Confirm calling and Teams requirements, configure the connections, then pilot representative scenarios. Client video tutorials and agreed support help your team learn and apply the workflow.

What affects scope and ongoing costs?

Users, number portability, call types, Microsoft and carrier licensing, recording services, AI usage and storage affect the scope. Confirm implementation, managed service and third-party charges in your Blueprint.
