HOW TO READ THE RESULTS

Transparent inputs.
Separate outcomes.

Cash comparison

Annual recurring difference = (current monthly cost − proposed monthly cost) × 12.

Period difference = annual recurring difference × years − one-off transition costs. Positive means a lower proposed cash cost; negative means a higher proposed cash cost.

Simple payback = transition cost ÷ positive monthly difference. This assumes immediate savings and excludes time value of money, price changes and unentered costs.

Capacity estimate

Annual hours released = weekly workflow hours × reduction percentage × working weeks.

Illustrative capacity value = hours released × loaded hourly cost. This is a way to value team time, not an additional cash return.

Test the whole workflow before accepting a time-saving assumption. Keep unknown costs visible rather than treating them as zero in a buying decision.
