Questions

Straight answers.

01What compliance evidence does Bunya keep?
File notes, approvals, documents and AI use, kept with the client record as the work happens, inside your firm’s own Microsoft 365.
02Does Bunya make our files compliant?
No. Compliance is your firm’s and your licensee’s judgement. Bunya keeps the evidence together, records approvals and flags gaps, so your team can make that judgement faster and show its working.
03Where is the evidence kept?
In your firm’s own Microsoft 365 environment, with the client record and its documents.
04Can we see where AI was used, and who approved it?
AI-usage recording can form part of the workflow and its evidence trail. Your scope defines which events are recorded, who can access them and how they are retained.
