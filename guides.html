Questions

Straight answers.

01Are the guides specific to Bunya?
Most are product-neutral. They cover the work, the checks and the questions to ask of any software. Where Bunya is relevant, the guide says so in its own section.
02Who are the guides written for?
Principals, advisers, paraplanners and practice managers in Australian advice firms, and anyone evaluating advice software.
03Do the guides include real client results?
No. Worked examples are illustrative and use fictional details. Customer results appear only in customer stories, with their source.
