Why Bunya exists

More Australians need advice. There aren’t more advisers.

The old answer to a growing client list was to hire. That answer is harder every year. The firms that grow now will be the ones whose systems carry the prework, so their advisers can carry the advice.

Bunya is shaped around the day-to-day work of Australian advice practices: the file notes, the fact finds, the SoA requests and the handovers between the first meeting and implemented advice.

What we build

One product at the centre. More when you’re ready.
