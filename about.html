01 / WHY BUNYA EXISTS

The work around advice
deserves attention too.

A client conversation is supported by preparation, records, documents, handovers and follow-up. The quality of those connections shapes the team’s day.

When information sits in different places, the team has to carry the context between them. Someone checks which record is current, remembers the next step or chases an update. Those small gaps can make otherwise straightforward work difficult to manage.

Bunya focuses on connecting that work. We start with how the firm operates, then shape the records, workflows and AI assistance around the responsibilities people need to fulfil.

Our ambition is practical: a practice where the team can see what matters, understand who owns it and move the work forward with confidence.

03 / WHAT GUIDES US

Principles you should
see in the work.

These are the expectations we bring to the design, delivery and ongoing use of a Bunya system.

01

Start with the real workflow.

Understand the people, information and exceptions involved before deciding what to automate. A clear handover matters as much as a faster task.

02

Keep people accountable.

AI can prepare drafts and proposed actions. Define who checks the output, what needs approval and how uncertainty is handled.

03

Make ownership practical.

Build in the firm’s Microsoft environment, with delivered components, ownership rights and handover defined in the agreement. Be clear about ongoing licences and support.

04

Prove the work together.

Use agreed scenarios to test the system with the people who will use it. Make gaps visible and give them an owner before rollout.

04 / WORKING WITH YOUR TEAM

Build understanding.
Then build the system.

A successful implementation needs clear decisions and people who know how to apply the result.

  1. Understand and define

    The Blueprint establishes priorities, scope, dependencies and responsibilities. Identify what will be built, connected or retained.

  2. Build and test

    Configure the agreed workflows, assess the data and test real operating scenarios with authorised sample information.

  3. Learn and apply

    Clients receive video tutorials to learn from and apply. Combine them with practical tasks so each role understands its work and handovers.

  4. Support and improve

    Agree support responsibilities, monitoring and how changes are requested. Keep the system useful as the firm’s needs develop.
