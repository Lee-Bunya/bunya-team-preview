Questions

Straight answers.

01How do we get started with Bunya?
With a free demo: one meeting to see Command Centre with example data and talk through how your firm works. If it looks like a fit, you receive a written proposal with scope options and pricing before any paid commitment.
02What happens in the demo?
We show you Command Centre with example data, and you tell us how your firm works today and where advice waits. It is one meeting, with no obligation.
03What is a Blueprint?
A paid, low-risk next step after your proposal: a working model of your firm’s workflows that your team clicks through before you commit to the build. It settles your stages, approval points, tools, documents, migration and running costs.
04How long does it take to go live?
Your timeline is set in your proposal and Blueprint, and released in stages so your team is using the first release while later ones are built.
05What if we decide not to go ahead?
You can stop after the demo, after the proposal, or after the Blueprint.
06How much of our team’s time does it need?
Mostly workshops during the Blueprint, then testing with a few representative users before go-live. Your Blueprint names the people and the time involved.
