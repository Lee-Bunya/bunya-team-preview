03 / BEFORE YOU MOVE

A transition plan.
Not a leap of faith.

Migration needs an assessment of your current setup, export access, data quality and dependencies. Agree the plan before committing to a cutover.

  1. Inventory what the firm relies on

    List records, documents, templates, modelling, research, reports, integrations and any licensee requirements. Identify who owns each requirement.

  2. Agree what stays and what changes

    Map each requirement to a demonstrated capability or a separately scoped connection. Do not assume Bunya replaces every Xplan module.

  3. Test an authorised data sample

    Confirm export rights and formats, map fields and relationships, assess document links, and validate duplicates and historical records.

  4. Prove the workflow with your team

    Test access, handovers, reports, exceptions and AI review steps. Define acceptance criteria and resolve gaps before rollout.

  5. Plan cutover and continuity

    Agree training, reconciliation, support, fallback arrangements and any period of parallel operation. Staging depends on technical and contractual feasibility.

Bring these to the first conversation

Your current modules and integrations, three important workflows, known data issues, licensee constraints and the outcomes you want to improve. Use sample information initially; agree a secure process before sharing client data.

04 / QUESTIONS BEFORE YOU SWITCH

Know the scope.
Understand the trade-offs.

A useful comparison ends with clear responsibilities, costs and a practical next step.

Is Bunya a complete replacement for Xplan?

That depends on what your firm uses. Bunya brings practice operations, campaigns and conversations into one operating system, but modelling, research, portfolio tools and other dependencies need individual assessment. The Blueprint identifies what is replaced, retained or connected.

Can Bunya integrate directly with our Xplan site?

Do not assume a ready-made connection. Access, available interfaces, permissions and commercial arrangements need to be confirmed with the relevant providers before an integration can be scoped.

Can we move all our history and documents?

The answer depends on export access, formats, record quality, document storage and mapping. Assess and validate a sample before agreeing the migration scope. Some items may require a different retention or access approach.

Is Bunya cheaper?

There is no universal answer. Compare actual proposals over the same period, including implementation, migration, licences, usage, support, retained software and internal effort. Do not assume time saved becomes a cash saving.

Do we have to switch everything at once?

A staged rollout may be possible, but it must account for data ownership, duplicate entry, integrations, licensing and support during the transition. The sequence is agreed after assessing your setup.

What does firm ownership mean?

Bunya is delivered in your firm’s Microsoft environment. Ownership terms, supplied components, documentation and handover are defined in the contract. Microsoft and third-party licensing and agreed support remain ongoing considerations.
