Questions

Straight answers.

01What is Broadcast HQ?
An optional add-on to Bunya Command Centre for marketing and client communications. You write an idea once, AI drafts each channel from it in your firm’s voice, and a person approves every variant before it is scheduled.
02Does content publish without review?
No. Every variant passes through your approval gate first. AI-prepared content is reviewed for accuracy, brand fit and the requirements that apply to your firm.
03Which channels can we use?
The supported channels and publishing accounts your Blueprint confirms. Bring your preferred channels to the demo so we can talk through the right setup.
04How is client information kept out of posts?
Before anything fans out to the channels, a privacy check looks for names, ages and dollar figures so your team can remove them.
05Do we need Broadcast HQ to deliver advice?
No. It is an optional add-on. Command Centre delivers advice without it.
