01 / THE SHORT ANSWER

Overlapping tasks.
Different starting points.

Claude for Financial Advisors works with existing tools. Bunya brings client operations, campaigns and conversations into a scoped Microsoft-based operating system. Both need evaluation against your actual workflows.

Prepared by Bunya, a competing provider. Sources checked 20 September 2026. This is a comparison of documented positioning and proposed scope, not a hands-on benchmark, security audit or feature-parity assessment.

Our assessment: a firm satisfied with its core systems may first test assistance across those systems. A firm changing client records, responsibilities and team-wide workflows has a broader implementation decision. Bunya’s case should rest on that scope and delivery model, rather than assuming Claude lacks actions or governance.

03 / CHOOSE BY THE PROBLEM

Keep the decision
specific to your practice.

Start with the work that is difficult today and the systems you intend to retain.

EVALUATE CLAUDE

Your core tools work well.

You want to reduce preparation and follow-up effort while keeping existing records and processes. Test connector coverage, permissions and the supported actions in your environment.

EVALUATE BUNYA

You want to change how the firm operates.

You need a defined home for client work, responsibilities, campaigns and conversations. Test the proposed build, migration plan and ongoing ownership responsibilities.

A combination may be worth assessing, but a direct Claude–Bunya integration is not established by this comparison. Confirm the technical connection, data handling, licensing and support before assuming they work together.

04 / RUN THE SAME SCENARIO

Follow one review
all the way through.

Use fictional or otherwise authorised sample information. Ask each supplier to show the work, including the exceptions.

  1. Prepare a client review.

    Find the relevant records and outstanding work. Identify the source of each important fact and what happens when information is missing.

  2. Correct a draft.

    Introduce an incorrect figure or an ambiguous instruction. Check how a reviewer corrects it and whether the correction reaches the saved record.

  3. Approve and assign follow-up.

    Show which actions need approval, where tasks are stored and who sees them after the AI session ends.

  4. Resolve a failed connection.

    Show what happens when access expires, a record is ambiguous or a write fails. Identify the support owner.

  5. Review the full investment.

    List retained tools, implementation, licences, usage, training and support over the same period. Use our total cost comparison checklist.

For Australian advice firms

Confirm Australian data sources, your required advice documents and licensee review processes. A US-oriented compliance workflow does not establish Australian suitability, and an Australian-focused build does not guarantee compliance. Ask for demonstrated coverage of your requirements.

05 / COMMON QUESTIONS

Ask about the details
behind the comparison.

The useful answer depends on your systems, permissions and delivery scope.

Is Claude for Financial Advisors just a chatbot?

No. Anthropic’s advisor offering packages workflows and connectors. Evaluate the available actions and controls instead of treating it as text generation alone. See the official plugin repository.

Does Bunya replace everything Claude connects to?

No such assumption should be made. Specialist research, modelling and portfolio requirements need individual assessment. The Blueprint identifies what is built, connected, retained or excluded.

Does Claude have audit and review controls?

Anthropic’s webinar describes approval and audit trails. The launch recommends Enterprise for registered investment advisers because of audit logging. Confirm plan-specific controls and retention for your firm. Webinar overview · Launch details.

Which is cheaper?

This page does not establish a price winner. Obtain complete quotes for equivalent requirements, including retained software and internal effort.

Can we use Claude with Bunya?

Potentially as part of a separately assessed design. Confirm supported access, permitted data flows and responsibility for actions. We are not claiming a ready-made integration.
