More questions, answered ↗
What makes up the Bunya operating system?

Command Centre runs the practice. Broadcast HQ supports campaigns, publishing and connected engagement. Bunya Voice connects business calls and Teams meetings to client context and follow-up. Together, these form the Bunya operating system.

What is included in our build?

Your Blueprint defines the products, users, workflows, integrations and support included for your firm. Microsoft licensing, usage and any third-party services are confirmed separately.

Where does human approval fit?

People review advice drafts, approve marketing content and review eligible meeting follow-up before it is sent. Access and approval rules are agreed as part of your configuration.

Is Broadcast HQ the same as Grow?

No. Broadcast HQ is the campaign and content product within the operating system. Grow is an optional service for brand, website, SEO and content support.

Can we see all three products?

Yes. Book a walkthrough of the complete system. You can also explore the interactive Command Centre demo before the meeting.
